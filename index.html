Questions

Things owners ask me

I already have Google, Yelp and Instagram. Why do I need a website?

Those pages belong to Google, Yelp and Instagram, and they show other businesses right next to yours. Your website is the one place that's all about you, made to look and feel like your business, with your hours, your services, your photos and a button to reach you.

Do I have to do anything technical?

No. I build it, host it and keep it updated. When you want something changed, you text me.

How long does it take?

Once you OK it, it's live in about 2 business days. If your package includes a photo shoot, it's live about 2 business days after the shoot.

What does the monthly cover?

Hosting, your domain, fixes and updates, and small changes like hours, holiday hours, promos and discounts, done within 2 business days.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, so it stays yours even if you leave. Every plan runs at least 3 months, and whenever it ends, you get a free copy of your site files.

What if I don't like it?

You don't pay anything until it's live and you like it. Not happy in the first month? You get everything back, setup included. Not happy in month 2 or 3? We refund your plan fees for months 2 and 3. After 3 months, cancel anytime.

Can you guarantee more customers?

No, and anyone who does is guessing. What I promise is a fast, good looking site that makes it easy for people to find you and reach you.

Not having a website is losing you customers and money.

It's 2026, it's time your business finally gets a website. You focus on your business, and we'll handle the rest.
