Matsu City privacy policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

What Matsu City is

Matsu City is a private business dashboard run by Jay Matsumoto. It is used only by Jay and by Nikk Amstadter, the owner of the YouTube channel Big Bear Weather and More. It is not a public app.

What it reads from YouTube

When the channel owner presses Connect YouTube and approves, Matsu City gets read-only access to the channel's:

  • public details, such as video titles, view counts, likes and comments counts;
  • YouTube Analytics, such as views, watch time, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources, audience age and country, and audience retention for each video;
  • estimated earnings reports.

It cannot change, upload, delete or comment on anything. It never sees the Google password.

What it does with that data

The numbers are shown on Jay's and Nikk's dashboard so they can see how the channel is doing. The data is stored only on Jay's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.

Matsu City's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Turning it off

The channel owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all reading. On request, Jay will delete the saved numbers.
